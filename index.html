About Me

I’m Ismail Halvi , a Junior Front-End Developer and Web Design and Coding graduate passionate about building modern, responsive, and user-friendly web experiences.

My journey into web development started with HTML and CSS , where I developed a strong interest in creating clean and responsive interfaces. I then expanded my skills by learning JavaScript, React, Tailwind CSS, Bootstrap, and Sass, while continuously improving my understanding of modern front-end development practices.

I graduated in 2026 with an Associate Degree in Web Design and Coding from Anadolu University. Throughout my studies and practical learning, I worked on different web projects that helped me strengthen my skills in responsive design, component-based development, API integration, and modern CSS frameworks.

Alongside my development journey, I gained practical technical experience as a Technical Student Supervisor at Cloud Soft , where I supported students with coding and technical problems, reviewed and corrected code, and helped them understand and solve web development issues.

I’m highly self-directed when it comes to learning and problem-solving. I rely on independent research to understand unfamiliar topics, investigate technical issues, and find solutions on my own before seeking assistance. I enjoy exploring reliable sources, comparing different approaches, and testing solutions until I understand the problem and find an effective way forward.

I also use AI-assisted development workflows as part of my learning and programming process. I’m comfortable writing structured and detailed prompts, providing the necessary context and constraints, and guiding AI tools toward the type of result I need. I then review, test, refine, and integrate the output into my work rather than relying on it without verification.

Today, I’m focused on continuously improving my front-end skills, building better projects, and gaining professional experience in a real-world development environment. Based in Turkey, I’m also open to remote opportunities, freelance projects, and collaborations.
